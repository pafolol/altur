    03 · SIDE BY SIDE

    The specialist and the phone-hardened model, on the same audio

    The specialist was fitted on the Altur recordings, where the caller channel is digitally silent between turns and the synthetic callers happen to be louder. A real telephone line erases both of those cues. Robust V2 is the same frozen backbone with a classifier that also trained on those calls after a line. Stage some audio above, tick the line simulation, and ask them both.

    03 · TEST DECK

    Listen to the held-out calls, then let it decide

    The 71 validation calls were never used to train the model (speaker-disjoint split from the organisers). Pick a call, listen, then ask - the detector receives the WAV exactly as it would from the judges. The true label stays hidden until the verdict is in.
