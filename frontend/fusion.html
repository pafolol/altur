01 — the mix

What each vote is worth

The faders are raw weights; only their ratio matters, so the big number is the share of the decision each layer actually holds. Both at the same value is an even split — the default, and the honest one. Every change is sent to the server's combiner, so what you see here is exactly what POST /detect answers - including for the judges. reset: even split puts every knob back to the shipped default.

02 — the held-out calls

Every system's score, then the verdict they agree on

The 71 validation calls, speaker-disjoint from everything either layer trained on. Each call is scored once per layer and the scores are cached, so moving a fader afterwards re-decides all 71 instantly without a model running. Rows the fusion gets wrong are flagged red. hide the truth blanks the labels if you would rather guess along.
