04 — how the confidence built

What it would have said, after each chunk

The classifier averages the log-odds of every chunk, so the average of the first k chunks is exactly what the deployed model would have answered having heard only those k — same aggregation, same calibration, nothing re-run. The horizontal bands are the decision threshold and the confidence band; the point where the line enters the band is the detection.
